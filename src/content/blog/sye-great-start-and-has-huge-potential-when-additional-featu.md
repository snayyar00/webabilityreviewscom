Did a basic test on a Webflow staging site and "it seems to work pretty well"; feels the product is good value for money. Flagged a missing important feature: beyond a simple accessibility score, the platform needed to tell users how to improve accessibility. The team replied that it is on the roadmap; he assumes it will be included for AppSumo users and asks to be told immediately if not. "The team seems to listen to customer feedback, so I hope... I look forward to this product flourishing."

The founder replied on Jul 14, 2025.
