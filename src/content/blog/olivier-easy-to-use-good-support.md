olivier.g is a verified purchaser and an AppSumo regular. This is one of the
earliest reviews in the set, from May 2025.

**What worked:** quick setup, page scanning with advice attached, and the
visitor-facing widget with controls such as adjustable font size. He notes
"quick reply to my questions from the support team."

**What did not, at the time:** not all of the widget was translated into other
languages, and the screen reader skipped some words.

He expected both to be fixed. Worth reading with the date in mind — this review
predates most of the others here by roughly a year, so it is a snapshot of an
earlier version of the product rather than a current one.

The founder replied two days later and acknowledged both gaps directly.
