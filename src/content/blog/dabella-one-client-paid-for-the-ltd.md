Dabella leases websites and runs SEO retainers. They bought the top tier at $295
and resold three years of ADA compliance to one client for $750 — covering the
whole lifetime deal on the first sale, with slots still spare.

What they call out as agency-relevant:

- Setup took **under 10 minutes**
- Multiple widget placements
- A silent background fix mode
- White-label, confirmed

Their wider argument is that bundling compliance into an existing retainer
improves client retention, because it adds a recurring reason for the client to
stay.

They also mention walking clients through IRS Form 8826, the disabled access
credit worth up to $5,000 — and explicitly flag that this is not tax advice.
Treat it the same way: talk to an accountant, not to a review page.
