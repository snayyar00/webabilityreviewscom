sushilkedia is a verified purchaser with more than 500 AppSumo deals behind
them, and ranks this one at the top.

They describe themselves as a non-coder running WordPress. At first they missed
that roughly 200 discovered auto-fixes were still pending and needed to be
applied. After contacting support and applying them, they report **"a 100 out of
100 Accessibility score on every page"** as measured by Google PageSpeed
Insights.

Their fair criticism: the help section should explain that step up front, so a
new user does not have to ask. That onboarding gap is the honest takeaway from
an otherwise five-taco review.
